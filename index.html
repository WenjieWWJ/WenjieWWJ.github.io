I am a Professor in University of Science and Technology of China. Prior to that, I was a postdoctoral research fellow in NExT++, working with Prof. Tat-Seng Chua and Prof. See-Kiong Ng in National University of Singapore. I obtained my PhD from NUS in 2023, and received my B.Eng. degree from Shandong University (Elite Class) in 2019, supervised by Prof. Liqiang Nie . My research interests include Personalized LLMs and Agent, Multi-modal Generation, Causal Reasoning, and AI4Science.
